8

The "8" Track (Figure-Eight)

Primary automated skill test for Two-Wheelers & Light Motor Vehicles (LMV)
Intermediate
Entry / Start Point
Driving Trajectory
Exit Point
Track Lane Width 2.70 Meters
Center Turn Radius 4.50 Meters
Speed Window 10–15 km/h
Max Time Limit 120 Seconds

Official Examination Checklist:

  • 1 Entry: Enter smoothly at the green marker in 1st gear. Maintain steady clutch friction without accelerating aggressively.
  • 2 Left Loop: Follow the outer perimeter of the first loop clockwise. Keep wheels centered; touching yellow boundary lines triggers automated sensor penalties.
  • 3 Crossover Transition: Straighten wheels momentarily at the center intersection before engaging opposite steering lock for the counter-clockwise loop.
  • 4 Zero Touchdown / Stall: For two-wheelers, zero foot-to-ground touches are permitted. For cars, engine stalling causes immediate retest.
H

The "H" Track (Reverse Bay & Parallel Maneuver)

Core test track for Car reverse control, parallel garage parking, and mirror discipline
Advanced
Forward 1st Gear
Reverse Gear S-Curve
Forward Exit
Bay Lane Width 3.00 Meters
Overall Track Length 12.00 Meters
Allowed Gear Shifts 1 Reverse Only
Sensor Threshold Zero Kerb Touches

Official Examination Checklist:

  • 1 Forward Entry: Drive forward into the left-top bay without touching either side boundary sensor. Come to a complete stop inside the designated stop box.
  • 2 Reverse Cross-Over: Shift into reverse. Using wing mirrors and rear-view mirror, back up and steer smoothly across the horizontal central crossbar into the right bay.
  • 3 Single Reverse Rule: The entire reversing maneuver must be done in one continuous fluid motion. Stopping and shifting back to forward gear to correct alignment fails the test.
  • 4 Exit: Shift into 1st gear and drive straight forward out through the designated green exit lane.
